### Learning & Practice

* [Lichess](https://lichess.org) - A free, open-source chess platform with online play, puzzles, studies, analysis tools, and tournaments.
* [Chess.com](https://www.chess.com) - The largest chess website offering games, lessons, puzzles, videos, and community features.
* [ChessKid](https://www.chesskid.com) - A beginner-friendly learning platform designed specifically for children and educators.
* [FIDE](https://www.fide.com) - The official website of the World Chess Federation, featuring ratings, regulations, and international events.
* [Internet Chess Club (ICC)](https://www.chessclub.com) - A long-running online chess server known for strong competition and instructional content.

### Analysis & Advanced Resources

* [Chess Tempo](https://chesstempo.com) - A powerful training site focused on tactical puzzles, endgames, and performance tracking.
* [365Chess](https://www.365chess.com) - A large database of master games with opening exploration and analysis features.
* [Chessable](https://www.chessable.com) - A training platform that uses spaced repetition to help players learn openings and strategic concepts efficiently.
* [The Week in Chess (TWIC)](https://theweekinchess.com) - A respected source for weekly game collections, tournament reports, and chess news.
* [Stockfish](https://stockfishchess.org) - One of the strongest open-source chess engines available for analysis and game improvement.
